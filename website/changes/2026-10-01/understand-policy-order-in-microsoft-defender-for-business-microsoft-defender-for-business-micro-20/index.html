1 October 2026Doc update+7 / −7 lines

In brief

The documentation now more clearly explains policy priority, how to change the order of custom policies, and that only the highest-priority applicable policy is applied.

What Defender admins need to know

Administrators can use the clarified guidance when reviewing policy order. Default policy priority remains fixed, while new policies are assigned priority and move default policies lower.
