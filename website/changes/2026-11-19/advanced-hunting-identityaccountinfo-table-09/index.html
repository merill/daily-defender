Advanced Hunting Identityaccountinfo Table

19 November 2026Feature update+12 / −9 lines

In brief

The table now removes two Defender risk fields, adds SourceProviderRiskLevelDetails, SourceSystem, and TimeGenerated, corrects field names, and changes SourceProviderRiskLevel from dynamic to string.

What Defender admins need to know

Review hunting queries or integrations that reference the changed, removed, or newly typed fields.

Summaries are generated from the documentation change itself.
