15 October 2026Doc update+2 / −2 lines

In brief

The Safe Links documentation date was updated, and its explanation of URL scanning before delivery, at click time, and through the client-side API was revised.

What Defender admins need to know

Review the updated guidance when assessing Safe Links behavior. No administrator action is stated.
