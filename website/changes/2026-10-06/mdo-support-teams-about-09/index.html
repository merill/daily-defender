In brief

The article now covers malicious activity in Teams messages, calls, and meetings, and lists messages, calls, meetings, and meeting participants as reportable items. It also links to Teams user reporting settings.

What Defender admins need to know

Administrators should use the updated guidance when reviewing Teams reporting settings. No action is required.
