Identity protection

Configure Scoped Access

17 November 2026Doc update+21 / −7 lines

In brief

The page now lists AccountInfo among available hunting tables, separates sensor management from health issue notifications, and documents availability for areas such as Domain page, Alert tuning, Identity Explorer, and Identity reports. It also explains visibility and device-onboarding behavior for AD domain-scoped users.

What Defender admins need to know

Use the updated matrix and scope rules when reviewing permissions and what delegated administrators can see or manage.
