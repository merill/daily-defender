Faq Defender For Servers

17 November 2026Feature update+27 / −2 lines

In brief

Starting November 1, 2026, machines are billed only when protected by Plan 1 or Plan 2 and in a billable power state. Plan 1 billing now distinguishes desktop devices from servers by operating-system and device information, with updated agent and classification rules.

What Defender admins need to know

Billing may change for existing machines, especially Plan 1 devices and systems that cannot be clearly identified as desktops. No mandatory administrator action is stated.

How are devices classified during Defender for Servers Plan 1 billing?

Microsoft Defender uses operating-system and device information to classify devices for licensing and billing. Classification doesn't depend on where a device is hosted, whether it's physical or virtual, or how you use it.

The following examples aren't exhaustive and don't confirm product support or licensing eligibility. Classification depends on device information, not the OS or distribution name alone.
