Automatic Attack Disruption Exclusions

4 October 2026Doc update+9 / −7 lines

In brief

The documentation now explains that identity exclusions affect automated response actions in both Defender for Identity and Defender for Endpoint. It clarifies Unified RBAC permissions for both products’ data sources and adds guidance on role assignments and data-source scope.

What Defender admins need to know

Administrators managing identity exclusions should review the updated permissions table and confirm their role assignments cover the relevant product data sources.
