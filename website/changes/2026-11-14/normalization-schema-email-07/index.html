Normalization Schema Email

14 November 2026Feature update+1 / −1 lines

In brief

The normalized email schema now lists `EmailInteraction` alongside `EmailDelivery` and `EmailInspection` as allowed `EventType` values.

What Defender admins need to know

Review validation, parsing, and downstream processing that rely on the documented EventType enumeration.
