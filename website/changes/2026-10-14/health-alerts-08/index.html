Health Alerts

14 October 2026Doc update+1 / −1 lines

In brief

The health alerts page revises the entry describing a missing or incorrectly applied Unified Sensor RPC Audit configuration tag and its resolution steps.

What Defender admins need to know

Administrators troubleshooting this alert should consult the revised verification guidance; no action is required solely because of this change.

Summaries are generated from the documentation change itself.
