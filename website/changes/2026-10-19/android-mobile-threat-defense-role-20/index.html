Android Mobile Threat Defense Role

19 October 2026Doc update+7 / −7 lines

In brief

The documentation now clarifies that the mobile threat defense connector is the Defender for Endpoint–Intune integration, separates assignment steps, and refers to the automatic-launch control as a switch.

What Defender admins need to know

Administrators get clearer guidance for assigning the role to supported Android Enterprise groups and enabling automatic Defender launch on COBO and COPE devices. No action is required.

Note

The Android MTD Role is an Android Enterprise platform role. It's different from the Microsoft Defender for Endpoint mobile threat defense capability. For an overview of the Defender for Endpoint mobile solution,more information, see Microsoft Defender for Endpoint mobile threat defense on Android and iOS.

Capabilities provided by the MTD Role

  • Defender for Endpoint is deployed to managed Android devices.
  • Microsoft Intune manages the target Android devices.
  • The Defender for Endpoint and Intune integration (theis configured. This element is the mobile threat defense connector) is configured.connector.
  • Devices are enrolled through the Android Management API with a supported Android Enterprise enrollment scenario.
  • Defender for Endpoint is assigned to devices as a required application.

For the enrollment scenarios that Defender for Endpoint supports on Android,scenarios, see Supported Android enrollment scenarios.

Supported enrollment scenarios

Deploy the Android MTD Role

The Android MTD Role is assigned through your device management solution. In Microsoft Intune, you grant the role to Defender for Endpoint through the Defender for Endpoint mobile threat defense connector, and thenconnector. Then target the configuration to supported Android Enterprise device groups. After the policy is deployed, the role is assigned automatically during device enrollment or through policy synchronization on existing managed devices.

For an overview of the role, see Mobile Threat Defense role for Android. For the connector toggle options that grant the role, see Mobile Threat Defense role in the Microsoft Intune documentation.

For the Defender for Endpoint connector, you can also turn on a separate toggleswitch that automatically launches Defender during setup on COBO and COPE devices. This toggleswitch requires that you grant the MTD Role permissions to Defender for Endpoint.

Verify MTD Role protections

After you assign the MTD Role is assigned to Defender for Endpoint, verify that the protections are active on a managed Android device:

  1. On the device, go to Settings > Apps > Microsoft Defender.
  2. Confirm that Microsoft Defender is exempt from Android battery optimization restrictions. On Android 14 and later, the battery optimization options in the app settings are unavailable and set to Unrestricted.
