Health Alerts

19 October 2026Doc update+7 / −7 lines

In brief

The guidance now specifies that automatic Windows event auditing applies to v3.x sensors and tells administrators to check the health issue table for the relevant sensor version before remediation. Several alert descriptions were also clarified.

What Defender admins need to know

Use the sensor-version guidance when investigating health alerts and selecting remediation steps; no immediate configuration change is required.
