Email and collaboration

Anti Spam Policies Configure

9 November 2026Doc update+3 / −3 lines

In brief

The documentation now states that the guidance applies when both the Defender for Office 365 and Exchange Online permissions toggles under Email & collaboration are Active.

What Defender admins need to know

Administrators reviewing RBAC access should verify both permissions toggles when applying this guidance; no required action is stated.
