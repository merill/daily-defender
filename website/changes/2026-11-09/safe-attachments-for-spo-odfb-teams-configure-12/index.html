9 November 2026Feature update+1 / −1 lines

In brief

The documentation now states that the Defender for Office 365 and Exchange Online permissions toggles under Email & collaboration must both be active for the unified RBAC configuration to apply.

What Defender admins need to know

Review these two permission toggles when configuring or troubleshooting access to the documented capability.
