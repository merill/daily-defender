Fixed Reported Inaccuracies

9 November 2026Feature update+30 / −0 lines

In brief

The fixed-inaccuracies report adds November 2026 entries, including MDVM support for six products and corrections to detection logic, vulnerability recommendations, tags, and product attribution.

What Defender admins need to know

Assessments and recommendations for the affected products may now be broader or more accurate; no administrator action is specified.

Summaries are generated from the documentation change itself.
