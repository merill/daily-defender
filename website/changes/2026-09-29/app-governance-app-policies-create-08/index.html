App Governance App Policies Create

29 September 2026New feature+18 / −21 lines

In brief

The documentation adds Risk management templates, including New high risk app and Risk score policies with customizable risk thresholds. It also updates monitoring guidance and the navigation path.

What Defender admins need to know

Administrators can use risk-based templates to generate alerts for high-risk apps. No configuration change or migration is required.
