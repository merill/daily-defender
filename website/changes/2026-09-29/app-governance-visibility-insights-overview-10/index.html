App Governance Visibility Insights Overview

29 September 2026Doc update+8 / −5 lines

In brief

The app categories documentation now includes Risky apps—apps with a high risk score—and adds Risk score. Minor wording was also clarified.

What Defender admins need to know

Administrators can use the documented category and score to focus app governance reviews on high-risk applications.
