Cloud and workloads

Manage Table Tiers Retention Isoc

24 November 2026Feature update+14 / −24 lines

In brief

The documentation now limits the stated 90-day included retention to supported Microsoft Defender data. It also notes source-dependent retention, marks several tables as Preview, gives January 3, 2027 as the expected date for a full 90-day history, and states that additional charges will apply based on ingestion and retention.

What Defender admins need to know

Review retention assumptions, supported sources, Preview table usage, rollout timing, and potential costs.
