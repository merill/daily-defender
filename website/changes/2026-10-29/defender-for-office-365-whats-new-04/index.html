Defender For Office 365 Whats New

29 October 2026New feature+4 / −3 lines

In brief

The page now documents the Defender XDR CallActivityEvents table, which contains details about activities performed during Microsoft Teams calls and meetings. Page metadata and taxonomy links were also updated.

What Defender admins need to know

Administrators can use CallActivityEvents to hunt and investigate Teams call and meeting activity.

October 2026

  • Hunt across activities in Microsoft Teams calls and meetings: The CallActivityEvents table in Microsoft Defender XDR advanced hunting contains details about activities performed during Microsoft Teams calls and meetings in your organization, helping security teams investigate suspicious call activity.
  • New ApproverUpn field in the EmailPostDeliveryEvents table: The EmailPostDeliveryEvents table in Microsoft Defender XDR Advanced Hunting now includes the ApproverUpn field. This field identifies the user or administrator who approved a remediation action, helping security teams investigate and audit post-delivery email actions more efficiently.
  • Expanding user reporting in Teams to include meetings: Users can report scheduled or Meet now Microsoft Teams meetings or individual meeting participants as a security concern during or after the meeting. Users can also report a meeting participant that was incorrectly identified as suspicious as not a security concern. Depending on Teams user reported settings, reported meeting and participant metadata is sent to the specified reporting mailbox, to Microsoft, or both.
