29 October 2026Doc update+3 / −1 lines

In brief

The page now documents hunting across Microsoft Teams call and meeting activities with the CallActivityEvents advanced hunting table and marks the capability as supported.

What Defender admins need to know

Administrators can use this documentation to investigate Teams call and meeting activity.

  • Remove users from Teams chat: To remediate malicious attacks, you can remove users from teams chats directly from the Teams message entity panel.

  • Hunting on Teams messages with URLs: You can hunt for Teams messages containing URL across three new advanced hunting tables: MessageEvents, MessagePostDeliveryEvents, and MessageURLInfo.

  • Hunting on Teams calls and meetings: You can hunt across activities performed during Microsoft Teams calls and meetings using the CallActivityEvents advanced hunting table.
