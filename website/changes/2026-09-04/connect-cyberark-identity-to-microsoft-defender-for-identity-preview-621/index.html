Connect CyberArk Identity to Microsoft Defender for Identity (Preview)

4 September 2026Doc updateAction required+7 / −7 lines

In brief

The Defender for Identity–CyberArk Identity article updates its prerequisites and RBAC terminology. It now states that users tagging identities as privileged accounts in the Microsoft Defender portal must also have the Privileged Cloud Auditors role.

What Defender admins need to know

Assign this role to the relevant user when privileged-account tagging is required for the integration.
