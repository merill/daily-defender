Pilot and deploy Defender for Office 365

This article provides a workflow for piloting and deploying Microsoft Defender for Office 365 in your organization. You can use these recommendations to onboard Microsoft Defender for Office 365 as an individual cybersecurity tool or as part of an end-to-end solution with Microsoft Defender XDR.Defender.

This article assumes you have a production Microsoft 365 tenant and are piloting and deploying Microsoft Defender for Office 365 in this environment. This practice will maintain any settings and customizations you configure during your pilot for your full deployment.

  • Quickly configure Microsoft Teams protection in Microsoft Defender for Office 365 Plan 2
  • Security Operations Guide for Teams protection in Microsoft Defender for Office 365
