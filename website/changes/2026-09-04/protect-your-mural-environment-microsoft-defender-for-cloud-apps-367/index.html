Protect your Mural environment | Microsoft Defender for Cloud Apps

4 September 2026Doc update+17 / −12 lines

In brief

The documentation now states that the Activity performed by terminated user policy requires Microsoft Entra ID as the identity provider and that Mural audit-log ingestion is delayed by 48 hours. It also includes wording, heading, and navigation updates.

What Defender admins need to know

Administrators should account for the identity-provider prerequisite and 48-hour delay when configuring and monitoring Mural in Defender for Cloud Apps.
