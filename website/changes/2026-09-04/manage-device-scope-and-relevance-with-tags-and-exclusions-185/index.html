4 September 2026Doc update+8 / −7 lines

In brief

The page now clarifies that excluded devices may take up to 10 hours to disappear from vulnerability management data, while restored data may take up to 8 hours to reappear after exclusion stops.

What Defender admins need to know

Administrators should allow these processing windows when managing device exclusion scope.
