Defender Portal

4 September 2026Doc update+1 / −1 lines

In brief

The overview now refers to Microsoft Threat Intelligence and describes capabilities in Microsoft Defender XDR and Microsoft Sentinel that aggregate and enrich threat data from multiple sources.

What Defender admins need to know

Administrators should use the updated terminology when referencing threat-intelligence capabilities.
