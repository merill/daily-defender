Manage sites in Microsoft Defender for IoT

4 September 2026Doc update+5 / −5 lines

In brief

The article title, metadata, and introductory wording were updated. The site assignment instructions now clarify that a manual site assignment remains until the device is reset, while automatic reassignment is prevented.

What Defender admins need to know

No action is required; administrators should use the clarified guidance when manually assigning devices to sites.
