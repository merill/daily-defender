Email and collaboration

Quarantine About

4 September 2026Doc update+4 / −2 lines

In brief

The page now documents that messages quarantined by Safe Attachments policies with unscannable encrypted attachments are retained for 30 days. Admins can release them without a password, while users can provide the password to rescan their own messages.

What Defender admins need to know

Admins can use these details when managing quarantined messages and advising users about password-protected attachments.
