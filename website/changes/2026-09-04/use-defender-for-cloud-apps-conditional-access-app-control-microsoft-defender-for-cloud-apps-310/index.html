In brief

The article now more clearly explains that Microsoft Entra apps are automatically available for Conditional Access app control and can also be manually onboarded. It also simplifies the prerequisite and setup-link wording and updates metadata.

What Defender admins need to know

Administrators can use the revised guidance to understand app availability and the required Microsoft Entra Conditional Access policy when configuring access or session policies.
