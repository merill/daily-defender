How to investigate anomaly detection alerts

MITRE ATT&CK coverage for anomaly detection alerts

To explain and make it easier to map the relationship between Defender for Cloud Apps alerts and the familiar MITRE ATT&CK Matrix, we've categorized the alerts by their corresponding MITRE ATT&CK tactic.
