In brief

The documentation received wording updates, clearer content-type links, expanded ARM terminology, and added guidance and a link for configuring data connectors. Support and content customization instructions were also clarified.

What Defender admins need to know

Administrators have clearer navigation and guidance for managing solutions, content, and data connectors.
