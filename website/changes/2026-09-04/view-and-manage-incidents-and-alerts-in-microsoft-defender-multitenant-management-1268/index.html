View and manage incidents and alerts in Microsoft Defender multitenant management

4 September 2026Doc update+13 / −13 lines

In brief

The article now more clearly explains viewing and managing incidents and alerts across tenants. Status changes, classification, tagging, and comments can span tenants, while assigning multiple incidents or alerts is limited to items from the same tenant.

What Defender admins need to know

No configuration change is required. Administrators should use the tenant-specific portal for detailed incident or alert actions and account for the same-tenant assignment limit.
