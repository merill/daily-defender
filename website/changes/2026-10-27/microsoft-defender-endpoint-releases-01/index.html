Microsoft Defender Endpoint Releases

27 October 2026Retirement+28 / −0 lines

In brief

The documentation adds macOS version 101.26092.0009 release notes, including improved policy reliability, antivirus performance and health reporting, EDR updates, and the retirement of Legacy Device Control v1.

What Defender admins need to know

Administrators should account for the Device Control v1 retirement; new health reporting helps verify Bluetooth permissions and Microsoft Defender system-extension configuration. No required action or deadline is stated.
