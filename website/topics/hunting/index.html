Microsoft Defender for Office 365: New email submissions data in Advanced Hunting

Message CenterMC1484009 on mc.merill.net ↗Stay informed

Microsoft Defender for Office 365: View approver details for remediation actions in Advanced Hunting

Hunting and detection
