Whats New

Identity protection

The Suspected identity theft (pass-the-hash) and Remote code execution attempt alerts will be retired at the end of November 2026. Monitoring alternatives are Microsoft Defender for Endpoint behavioral blocking and containment, or custom detections in Advanced hunting.
