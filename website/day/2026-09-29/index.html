Tuesday, 29 September 2026

Day in brief

Identity sensor v2.x guidance now requires a directory service account for full coverage

The strongest administrator-facing change is explicit DSA guidance for sensor v2.x deployments, including DeletedObjects access and specific cross-domain scenarios. Cloud Apps added focused guidance for reviewing service principals with Microsoft Entra RBAC roles and documented risk-based app policy templates. Other notable updates list WSLc support and clarify that disconnecting Foundry disables agentic code scanning without a replacement connection.

  • Identity · Identity protection
    Sensor v2.x deployments need a DSA for full coverage

    Updated identity guidance says sensor v2.x deployments should configure a Directory Service Account for full security coverage. A DSA is required to access the DeletedObjects container and for certain cross-domain and non-domain-controller scenarios.

  • Cloud Apps · Cloud and workloads
    Cloud Apps adds service-principal RBAC review guidance

    A new article explains how to use App Governance to filter, investigate, and assess Microsoft Entra RBAC roles assigned directly to service principals, including permissions, privilege levels, and risk signals. Access to App Governance data and the required App Governance role are prerequisites.

  • Cloud Apps · Cloud and workloads
    App Governance documents risk-based alert templates

    App policy guidance now includes Risk management templates such as New high risk app and Risk score policies, with customizable risk thresholds. The page also updates monitoring guidance and the navigation path.

  • Endpoint · Endpoint protection
    Defender for Endpoint lists WSLc workload support

    The Defender for Endpoint What's New page now lists support for WSL containers (WSLc), including protection and visibility into WSLc activity. Administrators can include WSLc workloads in protection and monitoring.

  • Exposure Management · Vulnerabilities and exposure
    Foundry disconnection disables agentic code scanning

    The Mdash Foundry guidance clarifies that disconnecting Foundry disables agentic code scanning unless another connection is provided. It also specifies that Standard deployments use each of the three models once and clarifies the required model deployments for the Foundry resource.
