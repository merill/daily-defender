Day in brief

Unified RBAC guidance now requires both Defender for Office 365 and Exchange Online toggles

The supplied period is documentation-led, with the strongest administrator impact concentrated in RBAC prerequisites and Microsoft Sentinel operating procedures. Multiple Office 365 policy articles now state that both the Defender for Office 365 and Exchange Online permissions toggles under Email & collaboration must be Active for the documented Unified RBAC scenarios. Sentinel guidance marks direct analytics-rule playbook invocation as deprecated, details unified portal data-management controls, and separates Azure RBAC from Defender unified RBAC for playbooks. The Exposure Management exemptions page is now explicitly labeled preview.

  • Office 365 · Email and collaboration
    Office 365 policy guidance standardizes a two-toggle Unified RBAC prerequisite

    The Connection Filter Policies guidance changes from requiring only the Defender for Office 365 toggle to requiring both the Defender for Office 365 and Exchange Online permissions toggles under Email & collaboration to be Active. The same prerequisite now appears across the supplied anti-spam, anti-malware, anti-phishing, outbound spam, quarantine, Safe Attachments, and Safe Links guidance.

  • Sentinel · Incidents and response
    Sentinel marks direct analytics-rule playbook invocation as deprecated

    Alert-trigger playbooks and their Microsoft Sentinel Alert triggers remain supported, but direct invocation from analytics rules is deprecated. The revised procedure uses automation rules for alerts generated by one or multiple analytics rules.

  • Sentinel · Cloud and workloads
    Sentinel documents unified portal tabs for data and retention management

    The guidance now covers connector and table management, transformations, and ingestion-health monitoring in the unified Defender portal through Overview, Connectors, and Tables tabs. It also specifies URBAC Data (manage) and equivalent workspace permissions, limits the experience to commercial cloud, and identifies table-management limitations.

  • Sentinel · Cloud and workloads
    Sentinel playbook guidance separates Defender and Azure permission models

    The article distinguishes Microsoft Defender SOC Logic Apps and generated playbooks from Sentinel connector Logic Apps. Logic App playbooks require appropriate Azure roles, while generated playbooks require Defender unified RBAC permissions and no Azure roles; investigation-page access is also documented.

  • Exposure Management · Vulnerabilities and exposure
    Exposure Management exemptions guidance now carries explicit preview labeling

    The Manage exemptions page title and heading now include “(preview),” making the preview designation explicit in the documented capability. Its custom metadata was also changed and an embedded screenshot was removed.
