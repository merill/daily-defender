Day in brief

Defender for Cloud posture controls enter preview while SAP guidance adds collection-health KQL queries

30 October was largely a documentation-maintenance day: all 1,248 recorded items were updates, and the representative changes are dominated by reordered, replaced, or added metadata links across Defender products. Two substantive exceptions stand out. Exposure Management and Defender for Cloud guidance identifies posture policies and recommendation exemptions as available in public preview for Azure environments. Separately, Sentinel’s SAP system health guidance now links to KQL queries covering incomplete collection windows, truncation markers, daily and projected monthly volume, hourly peaks, and high-volume accounts.

  • Exposure Management · Vulnerabilities and exposure
    Azure posture policies and recommendation exemptions enter public preview

    Exposure Management guidance says these capabilities are available in public preview for Azure environments. Administrators can evaluate Azure resources against the Microsoft Defender for Cloud baseline, create custom posture policies, and centrally manage accepted-risk exemptions with lifecycle tracking.

  • Sentinel · Cloud and workloads
    Sentinel SAP guidance adds queries for collection and volume analysis

    The Monitor SAP system health article now links to KQL queries for detecting incomplete collection windows and analyzing truncation markers, daily and projected monthly volume, hourly peaks, and high-volume accounts.
