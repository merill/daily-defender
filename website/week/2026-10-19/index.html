Week in brief

Defender for Cloud shifts Azure plan management to Defender portal with DWU billing

Defender for Cloud is the week’s main operational shift: portal-based plan management moves Azure environments toward scope-level configuration and Defender Workload Unit billing, while Direct onboarding applies tenant-wide rather than to pilot groups. Defender for Identity adds a 30-day opt-out window before sensor v3.x defaults activate; Defender XDR also starts a 30-day AadDeviceId transition, and a mid-November App Governance permission realignment is announced.

  • Defender for Cloud · Cloud and workloads
    Defender for Cloud moves plan control into the Defender portal

    The new model changes billing to Defender Workload Units and shifts configuration to tenant, management-group, or subscription scope. Plan replacements, consolidations, or measurement changes may apply; resource-level configurations are not preserved, and migrated settings become view-only in the Azure portal.

  • Defender for Cloud · Cloud and workloads
    Direct onboarding applies to every eligible non-Azure server in the tenant

    The procedure covers all eligible existing and future non-Azure servers in the same Microsoft Entra tenant, including the affected Azure Arc-connected population. Individual-server and pilot-group selection is not supported. Verify healthy Defender for Endpoint telemetry, a matching tenant, and the required roles before enabling it.

  • Identity · Identity protection
    Defender for Identity gives administrators 30 days to opt out of v3.x defaults

    Eligible organizations with an active Defender for Identity workspace receive a 30-day banner before sensor v3.x activation and Windows auditing are enabled by default, including settings currently set to Off. Selecting Opt out within 30 days retains the current settings.

  • Defender XDR · Hunting and detection
    Advanced Hunting replaces AadDeviceId with EntraIdDeviceId

    The AadDeviceId column is being replaced by EntraIdDeviceId. AadDeviceId remains available for 30 days, giving administrators a limited window to update affected hunting queries.

  • Defender XDR · General
    Defender for Cloud Apps will realign App Governance role permissions

    Starting in mid-November 2026, App Governance permissions for selected Microsoft Entra roles will change as access control aligns with Defender XDR. Cloud App Security, Compliance Administrator, and Compliance Data Administrator roles are affected.

For Defender administrators

Prioritize Azure plan-migration review, including DWU billing and the loss of resource-level settings. Do not enable Direct onboarding without checking its tenant-wide scope. Identity teams should monitor the v3.x banner and act within the 30-day opt-out period if required, while XDR teams update hunting queries during the schema transition. Review App Governance role assignments before the mid-November permission change.
